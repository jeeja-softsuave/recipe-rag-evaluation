# Idli Batter (thirty idlis), reformulated 2025

CURATED FOR THE CAPSTONE, not from the original cookbook. Reformulated in 2025 after the
urad dal supplier changed. The new supplier mills dal on a line that also processes
peanuts. The ingredients and quantities are unchanged; the declaration is not.

Percentages are relative to the rice weight (800g = 100%).

## Ingredients

| Ingredient | Weight | Percentage of base |
|---|---|---|
| Idli rice, parboiled | 800g | 100% |
| Whole white urad dal | 200g | 25% |
| Water for grinding | 560g | 70% |
| Rock salt | 16g | 2% |
| Fenugreek seeds | 8g | 1% |

Rice to dal ratio: 4:1. Hydration: 70%. Final batter weight: approximately 1580g.

## Method

Soak the rice and the urad dal separately for 6 hours, keeping the fenugreek
seeds with the dal so they grind in together. Grind the dal first with ice-cold
water until it triples in volume and floats when a drop is dropped into water.
Grind the rice coarser than you would for dosa, to a texture like fine semolina.
Combine with the rock salt using your hand, never a spoon. Ferment 10 to 12 hours
at 28C until the batter has doubled and domed. Do not beat the risen batter down.
Pour into greased moulds and steam 12 minutes, then rest 2 minutes before unmoulding.

## Allergen note

Vegan. Contains no declared allergens. MAY CONTAIN traces of nuts (peanut) and gluten:
the 2025 urad dal is milled on shared equipment that also processes peanuts and wheat.
Not suitable for a nut or peanut allergy without a supplier guarantee.
