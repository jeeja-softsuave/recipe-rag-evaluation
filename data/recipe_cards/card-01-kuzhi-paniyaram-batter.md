# Kuzhi Paniyaram Batter (two dozen)

Percentages are relative to the rice weight (600g = 100%).

## Ingredients

| Ingredient | Weight | Percentage of base |
|---|---|---|
| Idli rice, parboiled | 600g | 100% |
| Whole white urad dal | 200g | 33% |
| Fenugreek seeds | 6g | 1% |
| Rock salt | 14g | 2.3% |
| Water for grinding | 480g | 80% |

Rice to dal ratio: 3:1. Hydration: 80%. Final batter weight: approximately 1300g.

## Method

Soak the rice and the urad dal in separate bowls for 5 hours, with the fenugreek
seeds sitting in the dal. Grind the dal first with ice-cold water until it is
light and holds a soft peak, then grind the rice to a fine grain that still feels
slightly sandy between the fingers. Combine both with the rock salt and mix by
hand for two full minutes; the warmth of your palm starts the wild fermentation.
Keep the batter noticeably thicker than dosa batter, or the paniyarams will not
hold their domed shape. Ferment 8 to 10 hours at 30C until risen and pitted with
bubbles. Do not stir the risen batter, or you will knock out the gas that gives
the paniyarams their spongy centre. Spoon gently into a greased paniyaram pan,
cook 3 minutes on the first side, turn each ball with a skewer and give it
2 minutes more.

## Allergen note

Vegan and gluten-free with no top-14 allergens. Contains no dairy. If you add
asafoetida to the tempering, check the label: most Indian brands are bound with
wheat flour and would make this unsuitable for coeliacs.
