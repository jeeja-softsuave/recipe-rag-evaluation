# Sanna (sixteen steamed cakes)

Percentages are relative to the rice weight (500g = 100%).

## Ingredients

| Ingredient | Weight | Percentage of base |
|---|---|---|
| Idli rice, parboiled | 500g | 100% |
| Water for grinding | 250g | 50% |
| Grated coconut | 150g | 30% |
| Toddy, or yeast slurry | 120g | 24% |
| Rock salt | 10g | 2% |
| Cooked rice | 40g | 8% |

Hydration: 50% from grinding water, plus 24% from the toddy. Yield: 16 cakes.

## Method

Soak the parboiled rice 4 hours and grind it with the grated coconut and the
cooked rice, using only the 250g of water; the toddy brings the rest of the
liquid and doing this the other way round gives a batter too slack to hold a
cake. Fold the toddy in by hand rather than beating it, so the natural yeasts
survive. Add the rock salt last, because salt slows a toddy ferment noticeably
when it goes in early. Ferment 5 to 6 hours at 30C until the batter has risen by
about half and smells faintly of alcohol. Ladle into greased moulds, filling each
only three-quarters full since they climb, and steam 15 minutes. Test with a
skewer and rest 5 minutes before turning them out or they collapse.

## Allergen note

Vegan and gluten-free. Contains coconut. Traditional sanna uses palm toddy, which
is mildly alcoholic and unsuitable for anyone avoiding alcohol; a slurry of 3g dry
yeast in warm water is the standard substitute and keeps the recipe alcohol-free.
