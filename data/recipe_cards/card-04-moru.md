# Moru (spiced buttermilk, 1.6 litres)

Percentages are relative to the curd weight (400g = 100%).

## Ingredients

| Ingredient | Weight | Percentage of base |
|---|---|---|
| Thick sour curd | 400g | 100% |
| Water | 1200g | 300% |
| Shallots, crushed | 20g | 5% |
| Green chilli, slit | 12g | 3% |
| Ginger, crushed | 10g | 2.5% |
| Rock salt | 6g | 1.5% |
| Curry leaves | 4g | 1% |

Curd to water ratio: 1:3. Yield: approximately 1600g.

## Method

Start from curd that has already soured for a day past setting; fresh sweet curd
makes a flat moru with no lift to it. Whisk the curd smooth on its own before any
water goes near it, then add the water in stages, because pouring it in all at
once leaves lumps that never break up. Crush the shallots, ginger and slit chilli
together with the rock salt in a mortar until bruised but not pulped, and stir
them through. Leave it to stand 2 hours at room temperature so the aromatics
infuse and the culture keeps working. Do not boil it at any point; heat splits the
curd and the drink turns grainy. Bruise the curry leaves between your fingers as
you add them, then refrigerate before serving.

## Allergen note

Contains dairy from the curd, so this is unsuitable for a milk protein allergy.
Vegetarian but not vegan. The fermentation consumes much of the lactose and the
1:3 dilution reduces it further, so many lactose-intolerant people manage a glass,
but it is not lactose-free. Gluten-free as written.
