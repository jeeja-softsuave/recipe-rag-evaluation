# Neeragaram (fermented rice water, four servings)

Percentages are relative to the cooked rice weight (300g = 100%).

## Ingredients

| Ingredient | Weight | Percentage of base |
|---|---|---|
| Cooked rice, cooled | 300g | 100% |
| Water | 900g | 300% |
| Shallots, sliced | 20g | 7% |
| Green chilli, slit | 8g | 2.7% |
| Rock salt | 3g | 1% |

Rice to water ratio: 1:3. Yield: approximately 1100g.

## Method

Use rice cooked earlier the same day and cooled completely; warm rice sours too
fast and turns sharp rather than pleasantly sour. Cover it with the water in an
earthenware pot, leaving the lid resting loose so gas can escape. Ferment 6 to 8
hours at ambient room temperature, which is all it takes in a warm kitchen. Add
no salt during the ferment, because salt slows the wild culture; the rock salt
goes in only at serving. When it is ready the water is cloudy and smells faintly
sour, and the grains have softened and swollen. Mash a portion of the rice into
the liquid with your fingers to thicken it, then stir in the sliced shallots, the
slit chilli and the rock salt. Serve at room temperature the same day; by the next
morning it is unpleasantly acidic.

## Allergen note

Vegan and gluten-free with no top-14 allergens. Contains no dairy. Because this is
a spontaneous wild ferment held at room temperature with no salt, discard any
batch that smells putrid rather than sour, or that grows surface mould.
