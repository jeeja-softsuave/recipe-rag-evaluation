# Kallappam Batter (twelve appams)

Percentages are relative to the rice weight (500g = 100%).

## Ingredients

| Ingredient | Weight | Percentage of base |
|---|---|---|
| Raw rice, pachari | 500g | 100% |
| Water for grinding | 420g | 84% |
| Grated coconut | 100g | 20% |
| Cooked rice | 50g | 10% |
| Sugar | 10g | 2% |
| Fine salt | 8g | 1.6% |
| Active dry yeast | 3g | 0.6% |

Hydration: 84%. Final batter weight: approximately 1080g.

## Method

Bloom the yeast with the sugar in a little water at 38C and leave it 10 minutes
until it froths; water hotter than that kills the yeast outright and the batter
will never rise. Soak the raw rice 4 hours, then grind it with the grated coconut
and the cooked rice to a smooth, pourable batter about the consistency of thin
buttermilk. Stir the bloomed yeast and the fine salt through. Because this is
yeast-leavened rather than wild-fermented, it needs only 4 to 5 hours at 28C —
far less than a wild batter, and it will go sour and flat if left overnight. The
batter is ready when the surface is domed and covered in fine bubbles. Pour a
ladleful into a hot greased appachatti, swirl once so the centre stays thick and
the edges run thin, cover, and cook 3 minutes without turning.

## Allergen note

Vegan and gluten-free. Contains coconut, which is a fruit rather than a true tree
nut, but name it for guests who avoid coconut because it carries the flavour here.
Contains yeast, which matters for anyone on a yeast-free diet.
