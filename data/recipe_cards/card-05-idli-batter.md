# Idli Batter (thirty idlis)

Percentages are relative to the rice weight (800g = 100%).

## Ingredients

| Ingredient | Weight | Percentage of base |
|---|---|---|
| Idli rice, parboiled | 800g | 100% |
| Whole white urad dal | 200g | 25% |
| Water for grinding | 560g | 70% |
| Rock salt | 16g | 2% |
| Fenugreek seeds | 8g | 1% |

Rice to dal ratio: 4:1. Hydration: 70%. Final batter weight: approximately 1580g.

## Method

Soak the rice and the urad dal separately for 6 hours, keeping the fenugreek
seeds with the dal so they grind in together. Grind the dal first with ice-cold
water until it triples in volume and floats when a drop is dropped into water;
this aeration, not the fermentation, is what makes idlis light. Grind the rice
coarser than you would for dosa, to a texture like fine semolina. Combine with
the rock salt using your hand, never a spoon. Ferment 10 to 12 hours at 28C until
the batter has doubled and domed. Resist the urge to beat the risen batter down,
because the trapped gas is the entire leavening. Pour into greased moulds and
steam 12 minutes, then rest 2 minutes before unmoulding or the idlis tear.

## Allergen note

Vegan and gluten-free with no top-14 allergens. Contains no dairy and no coconut.
Parboiled rice and urad dal are frequently milled on shared equipment, so buy
certified gluten-free grain if you are cooking for coeliacs.
